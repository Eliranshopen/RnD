## 3. Our assets and their provenance (every row in `v2/media/manifest.json`)
- **Hero:** `fixtures/rainbow/landing/hero-{1080,720,portrait}.mp4` + posters (reused as is; AI: Kling v2v + Topaz).
- **Floor 16:** the hold's stop pose = Kling frame 91 of `research/…/flyover3d/data/holdarc/jpg_hi/`. Floor 16's position is not drawn by eye: apartment 846's terrace point (`site_nodes.json` `apartment_hotspot_world` 10.325, -11.148, 1.0) projected through `holdarc/camera.json` frame 91 = (0.4979, 0.7883) of the frame, band ±1.6 m = y 0.7565–0.8201 (floor-to-floor 3.2 m, as the hold's picker). It matches where production's hold puts the «846» pill (screenshot of prod `/hold/`, read-only). Band width = the tower's width in that frame (0.14–0.60).
- **Cutaway:** a still of the live dollhouse `tour360/dist/apt846/3d/dollhouse.html` (preset 3d, its UI and labels hidden, transparent background) captured in headless Chrome; the live one opens on request in a dialog.
- **Rooms:** 8 flat straight-ahead views (`ffmpeg v360`, h_fov 84 wide / 62 tall, no edit) of the **gated** panoramas `tour360/dist/apt846/panos/*.webp`; yaws picked by eye from an 8-yaw contact sheet per room. The higher-resolution `apartments/apt846/_local/full/` set was NOT used: it differs from the gated set (PSNR 36–46 dB, kitchen/dining/living regenerated later).
- **Closing band:** Eliran's render THE-2 (`landing/renders/the-2-*`), badge «הדמיה».
