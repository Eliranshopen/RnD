# Eliran's rules for every image prompt and every check

Put the relevant sentences into every image prompt, and check each one by eye after.

1. **Same picture, one change.** "Return the SAME panorama, identical in everything … Change ONLY this: …". Name the target by what
   surrounds it ("the tall white flush door with a black lever just LEFT of the white pillar"), and say what must stay ("the narrow
   door right of the pillar and the console stay exactly as they are").
2. **The plan decides.** No door, opening, niche, wall or window the plan does not draw. A niche drawn open stays open. If the plan
   draws a symbol (a circle in a square = a washer place), ask Eliran before you put an object there. apt846: he wanted the niche
   EMPTY.
3. **No skirting boards:** "There are no skirting boards anywhere and none may appear."
4. **Doors:** interior doors are white and flush. The front door is a WHITE ZERO-LINE door with one slim matte black lever and a
   peephole, the same from every tab.
5. **Keep the projection:** "a 360-degree equirectangular panorama (2:1; its left and right edges meet behind the camera) … the left
   and right edges of the result join seamlessly".
6. **Same width:** an edit must not widen or move the element. Measure the polygon before and after. Paste only the element's
   polygon back (polygon_paste.py), never the whole answer.
7. **Every floor set:** a change made on one floor set is pasted onto all of them (base pick + parquet + concrete + marble), so
   the room is identical across floors.
8. **Check at the visitor's zoom:** take screenshots from the live tour at 1440×900 and 390×844, turned to the element.
9. **Disclosure:** the «הדמיה» badge stays on every AI picture.
